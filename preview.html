Pesto Chicken Meatballs

Easy, freezer-friendly chicken meatballs with pesto, gluten-free breadcrumbs, and almost no fuss.

Ingredients

  • 2 lb ground chicken
  • 1 small jar pesto — choose your favorite
  • About ½ bag Schär gluten-free breadcrumbs
  • 2 eggs
  • Salt and pepper, to taste
♥ Use your heart

Salt, pepper, and honestly the breadcrumbs too. You’re looking for a mixture that holds together nicely without getting dry.

Instructions

  1. Mix the base.

    Mix the pesto, breadcrumbs, eggs, salt, and pepper together until they’re well combined.

  2. Add the chicken.

    Add 1 pound of chicken and mix thoroughly before adding the second pound. Get in there with your hands and make sure everything is really well incorporated.

  3. Scoop and roll.

    Use some kind of scoop or measuring cup so the meatballs stay roughly the same size and cook evenly. The exact size doesn’t matter — larger meatballs just need more cooking time.

  4. Freeze.

    Freeze on a parchment-lined cookie sheet for at least a couple hours, then transfer to a freezer bag or container.

  5. Cook.

    Air fry at 425°F for about 20 minutes, until the centers reach 165°F. Cooking time varies with size and air fryer.

Notes & tips

  • Uniform meatballs cook more evenly.
  • Giant ¼-cup meatballs need more time.
  • A few hours uncovered in the freezer will not ruin them.

Storage

  • Flash-freeze first.
  • Transfer to a freezer bag or container.
  • Cook from frozen; no thawing needed.
