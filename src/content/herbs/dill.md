---
title: Dill
description: anethum graveolens
tags: []
flavorProfile: Dill has a fresh, grassy, and slightly sweet aroma.  Dill weed
  has a fresh, grassy flavor with a hint of citrus and anise, while dill seeds
  are bolder with more pronounced anise and citrus flavors.
culinaryUses:
  - Dill is particularly delicious when paired with fish.
  - Traditionally the seasoning of choice for pickles.
traditionalUses: Dill is a widely used remedy for gas and colic, and is
  especially great for children. Dill can also increase the production of breast
  milk, and help soothe a colicky baby. Dill is great for calming digestion,
  helping to relieve spasms and cramps.
growingNotes: Dill is native to southern Europe and central and southern Asia,
  growing wild in open areas.    Dill grows great for us in the US in mild
  weather, but frosts and heat are not friends. Grow in fertile, well draining
  soil in cool weather.
storage: Fresh dill is fantastic and flavorful. Store your fresh dill in a jar
  with some water, like a bouquet of flowers, in the refrigerator.  You could
  cover with a plastic bag, if you like, but just hanging in the fridge is also
  fine.  Change the water every other day, or so.  Dry dill should be stored in
  an air-tight jar with the rest of your kitchen herbs and spices.
cautions: Consumption of dill can cause sensitivity to sunlight.  This can also
  occur after contact with the sap or juices of the freshly cut plant.
featured: true
