title: Garlic
description: allium sativum
tags: []
flavorProfile: Garlic has a complex flavor profile, from sharp and spicy when
  raw to a rich sweet and savory nutty flavor when sauteed or roasted.
culinaryUses: []
traditionalUses: Garlic has long been used for its healing powers. Before
  antibiotics, it was a treatment for many types of infections, from
  tuberculosis to typhoid.  It was also used to dress wounds in World War I.  It
  has also been known to aid in reducing elevated blood pressure and cholesterol
  levels.
growingNotes: "Garlic was originally found in Asia, but today it is grown and
  cultivated all across the world.  Growing garlic is easy, and the many
  different types available make it possible for backyard gardeners around the
  world to grow their own. "
storage: There are so many different ways you can store garlic!  Many gardeners
  store their harvested garlic braided and hanging in a cool, dark location (but
  not in the refrigerator!).  Garlic could also be dried and powdered to store
  with the rest of your kitchen herbs and spices.  Since fresh is best, keeping
  your store bought cloves in a paper bag in a cool, dark place will help them
  stay fresh longest.
cautions: High amounts of garlic can increase the effects of medications
  affecting the blood, such as blood thinners and even aspirin.  Please consult
  your doctor before using garlic therapeutically.
