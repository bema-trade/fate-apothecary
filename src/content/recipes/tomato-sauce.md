title: Tomato Sauce
description: Flavorful tomato sauce for pasta, pizza, and more...without the
  extra ingredients.
category: Sauce
tags:
  - gluten free
  - dairy free
prepTime: 5 minutes
cookTime: 4+ hours
ingredients:
  - externalAffiliate: false
    ingredient: 28 ounce can petite diced tomatoes
  - externalAffiliate: false
    ingredient: 12 ounce can tomato paste
  - externalAffiliate: false
    ingredient: 1/4 cup concentrated bone broth
  - externalAffiliate: false
    ingredient: 1 tablespoon garlic powder
  - externalAffiliate: false
    ingredient: 1 tablespoon onion powder
  - externalAffiliate: false
    ingredient: 1 tablespoon dried basil
  - externalAffiliate: false
    ingredient: 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  - externalAffiliate: false
    ingredient: salt & pepper
tools:
  - instant-pot
instructions:
  - title: Combine ingredients.
    text: Add the canned tomatoes (do not drain them, you need the liquid!), canned
      tomato paste, bone broth, and all seasonings to the liner of your instant
      pot, your slow cooker, or a stock pot on the stove.  Mix well to combine.
  - title: Let it simmer.
    text: Let your sauce hang out on low heat for at least 4 hours.  In the instant
      pot, I use the "slow cook - high" setting.  Use high in a slow cooker or
      low on the stove.
useYourHeart: I do not measure anything in this recipe.  This is the original
  "my ancestors tell me when to stop."  I'll measure someday, just once, but I
  haven't yet.
notes:
  - Use whatever you have to cook the sauce in!  What's important is that it has
    time to cook low and slow, no matter the method.
storage:
  - Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to a week.
  - We like to use old pickle jars to store our sauce.
serving: We use this sauce for pizza, spaghetti, dipping mozzarella sticks...
