---
title: Everything Roasted Cashews
description: A snack with a flavor punch so big you'll forget they're healthy.
category: Snack
tags:
  - gluten free
  - dairy free
yield: 3 cups
prepTime: 5 minutes
cookTime: 22 minutes
temperature: 300°
ingredients:
  - ingredient: 1 egg white
  - ingredient: 3 tablespoons everything seasoning
  - ingredient: 2 tablespoons powdered monkfruit
  - ingredient: 3 cups unroasted, unsalted (RAW) cashews
instructions:
  - title: Preheat
    text: Get your oven heating up to 300°.  Grab a rimmed cookie sheet and line it
      with a piece of parchment paper.  Set this aside while you prepare the
      cashews.
  - title: Prep
    text: Using a hand mixer, beat the egg white until it is foamy and no longer
      translucent. Add the cashews, everything seasoning, and monkfruit and stir
      until well combined.  The seasonings should stick pretty well to the
      cashews.
  - title: Time to roast!
    text: Pour your seasoned cashews onto the prepared cookie sheet. Spread the nuts
      out so they're in a single layer and you've given them as much room as
      possible. Bake for about 22 minutes, until the cashews are just starting
      to show signs of roast.
  - title: Cool Down
    text: Remove the roasted cashews from the oven and leave them to cool
      completely. This is important.  The seasoning will come off if you disturb
      them before the egg white has cooled and set all the way.
  - title: Break It Up
    text: Once the cashews have cooled completely, you can lift the parchment paper
      and break up the cashews that have stuck together.  Store in an airtight
      container at room temperature.
useYourHeart: We do not add salt to these but if your everything seasoning
  doesn't contain salt, you'll want to add some.
notes:
  - Your friends will love you if you feed them everything roasted cashews.
storage:
  - Store in an airtight container for up to one week.  We've stored in
    everything from glass jars to plastic zip bags.  Do what works best for your
    family!
serving: Be careful, they disappear faster than you think they will!
