---
title: Pesto Chicken Meatballs
description: Easy, freezer-friendly chicken meatballs with pesto, gluten-free breadcrumbs, and almost no fuss.
category: Main dish
tags:
  - Gluten Free
  - Freezer Friendly
  - Air Fryer
yield: About 35 meatballs
prepTime: About 10 minutes
cookTime: About 20 minutes
temperature: 425°F
timerMinutes: 20
ingredients:
  - 2 lb ground chicken
  - 1 small jar pesto — choose your favorite
  - About 1/2 bag Schär gluten-free breadcrumbs
  - 2 eggs
  - Salt and pepper, to taste
instructions:
  - title: Mix the base.
    text: Mix the pesto, breadcrumbs, eggs, salt, and pepper together until they're well combined.
  - title: Add the chicken.
    text: Add 1 pound of ground chicken and mix thoroughly before adding the second pound. Get in there with your hands and make sure everything is really well incorporated.
  - title: Scoop and roll.
    text: Use some kind of scoop or measuring cup so the meatballs stay roughly the same size and cook evenly. The exact size doesn't matter — just remember that larger meatballs will need more cooking time. Roll each portion into a meatball.
  - title: Freeze.
    text: Line a cookie sheet that fits in your freezer with parchment paper. Arrange the meatballs on the sheet and freeze for at least a couple of hours, until they're firm enough that they won't stick together. Transfer them to a freezer bag or container until you need them.
  - title: Cook.
    text: Air fry at 425°F for about 20 minutes, until the centers reach 165°F. Cooking time will vary with meatball size and your air fryer, so use an internal thermometer to be sure.
useYourHeart: Salt, pepper, and honestly the breadcrumbs too. You're looking for a mixture that holds together nicely without getting dry.
notes:
  - Any scoop or measuring cup works — uniform meatballs cook more evenly.
  - Larger meatballs, including the gloriously gigantic 1/4-cup variety, will need more cooking time.
  - Yes, they can sit uncovered on the tray in the freezer for a few hours. They will survive.
storage:
  - Freeze after flash-freezing on a tray.
  - Store in a freezer bag or container.
  - Cook from frozen; no need to thaw.
serving: We usually serve these with gluten-free pasta and vegan Alfredo. Vegan Alfredo recipe coming eventually because apparently I'm documenting the whole kitchen now.
